# Bridgecom data accuracy audit

Carried out on 9–10 October 2026 (Jordan time), before the AI Quest submission. The audit started from the existing data, checked it against official sources, and fixed the riskiest problems before adding anything new.

- Per-record tables, the full revision log and validation output are in [09-data-audit-tables.md](09-data-audit-tables.md). They are generated by `npm run audit:data`.
- Every count below was measured from `data/verified/*.json` (`src/lib/data/coverage.json`). None is an estimate.

**How the sources were read.** Only the WebFetch tool could reach sources; `curl` is blocked in the build environment. Several official sites refused automated access on 9 October 2026, so nothing from them was used:

- mohe.gov.jo (most pages), admhec.gov.jo, moe.gov.jo, petra.gov.jo
- ju.edu.jo (University of Jordan)
- the Mutah University admission brochure

Where only news reports were available, they are labelled as news.

## A. Data accuracy audit: what was wrong and what changed

### Highest-risk findings (fixed)

1. **Outdated admission minimums could tell a student they were eligible when they were not.**
   - University lists from 2024 show medicine at 85% (JUST, Yarmouk). A Higher Education Council decision raised medicine and dentistry to **90% from 2025/2026** at all universities.
   - Another decision set **75% for IT and AI faculties from 2026/2027**. JUST's 2024 list shows 65%.
   - Before the fix, a student with 87% saw "Meets minimum" for JUST medicine.
   - The fix is a national-rules layer (`data/verified/admission-rules.json`). When a newer national minimum is higher than the university's older list, the result now reads **"Check: newer minimum reported"**, with the rule, its start year and its source.
   - Both rules come from news reports (Roya News, corroborated by Al-Rai and Khaberni), because mohe.gov.jo could not be opened. They are labelled "news report of an official decision".
2. **Unlabelled academic years.**
   - JUST fees were marked "2024", but the document never states a year; only its file name does. They are now labelled "2024 (from file name)".
   - Hashemite's file is named "_2027", but its text has no year, so no year is shown.
   - Results now say "fee year not stated" instead of showing nothing.
   - **None of the 44 stored fees is confirmed for 2025/2026 or 2026/2027.** The newest dated fee table is Yarmouk's 2024/2025.
3. **Unconfirmed values were removed instead of kept.**
   - The 70% minimum and 132 credit hours shown for Philadelphia's CS, Software Engineering and Data Science & AI programmes could not be tied to those programmes in the brochure (the "70% 132" text belongs to Applied Chemistry). They are now withheld, which affects 6 values.
   - Philadelphia prices each credit hour by Tawjihi band, so no single fee is stored.
4. **A suspended programme was offered.** Yarmouk's 2024/2025 list marks Business Administration "موقوف" (admission suspended). It was removed.
5. **Fee tracks were ambiguous.**
   - Hashemite's PDF has three unlabelled price columns. The stored fee is the lowest column, now labelled "regular (inferred)".
   - JUST's and Al al-Bayt's columns were confirmed as the regular programme.
6. **Wrong university type.** MoHE lists Al Hussein Technical University (and WISE) as operating **under a special law**, not as private. The schema and labels now support this.
7. **Scholarships had no application status.** None of the 13 records said whether it was open.
   - Every record now has a status (open, announced, closed, historical or unverified) with its window and academic year.
   - The engine re-checks the status against today's date. An "open" record whose closing date has passed shows as **closed**, and a closed or historical opportunity can never be marked "Your answers fit".
   - **No opportunity in the dataset is open on 10 October 2026.**
8. **Secondary sources.**
   - Wikipedia was the type and location source for 10 universities. It has been replaced by the MoHE university lists or the universities' own pages.
   - A 2019 Jordan Times article was the only source for the teachers' children makruma. It has been replaced by 2026 Jo24 reports, still news only and flagged as such.

## D. AI reliability improvements

The matching now runs in five layers, A to E.

- **A. Retrieval:** facts come only from `data/verified`. The AI never supplies a fee, minimum or eligibility rule.
- **B. Eligibility:** a rule check against the programme's published minimum and stream, plus the newer national minimums.
  - The outcomes are: meets the minimum; below the minimum; check (a newer minimum is reported); unknown (no minimum); unknown (sources disagree).
  - This is kept separate from fit. A past competitive cut-off is shown as history only, never as the minimum.
- **C. Fit:** transparent weights that students can adjust with sliders. Criteria without data are left out instead of scored zero, and the share of information available is shown next to the score.
- **D. Explanation:** the AI words an explanation from the engine's facts only. It is rejected and replaced by a template if it contains any number, or now **any link or web address**, that is not in the facts. The prompt now also forbids estimating a missing cost and asks for the fee year.
- **E. Attribution:**
  - Every result shows its sources and check date, the fee year (or "fee year not stated"), and the national rule with a link.
  - A missing cost reads: "Information not currently verified. Please check the university's official website."
  - Scholarship cards show the status, deadline, academic year and check date.

**Validation.** `npm run validate:data` runs in the unit tests and blocks any of the following:

- duplicate universities or programmes
- fees outside plausible JOD ranges per track (catches US dollars or semester totals)
- implausible totals
- missing or non-https sources
- a fee without a track
- "open" scholarships without dates or past their deadline
- contradictory minimums or fees

Missing academic years and news-only sources are listed as warnings.

## I. Outstanding limitations

- **Fees are not current.** No 2025/2026 or 2026/2027 fee table was found for any university. Costs are labelled with the year the document states, or "not stated".
- **National minimums come from news.** They are reports of HEC decisions, not the decisions themselves. The IT/AI rule's sector (public, private or both) is not stated.
- **Competitive cut-offs** exist only for medicine and pharmacy at three universities. None was found for CS, SE, AI, nursing or civil engineering.
- **Missing data:**
  - No data for the University of Jordan, Mutah, Al-Balqa and others because sites blocked access.
  - No accreditation status anywhere.
  - Credit hours missing for 7 Hashemite and 5 Al al-Bayt programmes.
- **Inferred readings:**
  - The Hashemite fee track is inferred.
  - Yarmouk's figures were split from run-together PDF text; a USD column cross-check supports the split.
  - MEU's fee column is not labelled "per credit hour".
- **Scholarships:** 9 records have unverified dates. Princess Muna and Royal Court criteria pages could not be opened.
- **Ministry restructuring:** reports suggest MoHE has merged into a "Ministry of Education and Human Resource Development". This was not confirmed, and names are kept as published on the pages read.

## J. Competition readiness: what to say

**Can be demonstrated with confidence:**

- Every number on a result traces to a stored record with its source and check date.
- Eligibility is a rule check, kept separate from fit, and it catches newer national minimums.
- Missing data stays missing and is shown as missing.
- Closed scholarships are never recommended as current.
- AI wording is checked for invented numbers and links.

**Must be qualified:**

- "A sourced subset of 49 programmes at 8 universities, with fees from 2024/2025 or undated documents, not current-year prices."
- "A directory of 26 universities from the MoHE lists, not every institution."
- "National minimums as reported in the news; confirm on admhec.gov.jo."
- "No scholarship in the dataset is open today."
- "Not tested with students."

**Do not claim:**

- complete national coverage
- 2026/2027 fees
- accreditation checks
- any admission probability
